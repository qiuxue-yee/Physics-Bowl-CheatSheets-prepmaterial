A reading route / 02

Learn. Try. Return.

A simple way to move between an idea and the question that puts it to work.

  1. 01 /

    Start with an idea.

    Choose a topic in the guide. Read the concept, its conditions, and the common pitfalls.

  2. 02 /

    Give it a try.

    Work an example or a linked problem on your own before comparing the solution.

  3. 03 /

    Find your way back.

    When a step is unclear, follow the link to revisit the relevant unit and its assumptions.

Behind the pages / 03

A work in
progress, thoughtfully.

This revision began with a question: how can a collection of notes become easier to navigate and more reliable to use? The sources are now organized by unit, the books link to each other, and selected questions point to their original AAPT exams.

The review also corrected mismatched figures and several derivations. The record is open about what was checked and what still needs attention.
